What we do

Check-ups and cleaningA full exam, X-rays when needed, and a cleaning. About 45 minutes.
FillingsTooth-colored fillings, done in one visit with numbing that actually works.
WhiteningIn-clinic or take-home kits. We tell you honestly what to expect.
Kids' dentistryShort, friendly visits from age two. Stickers are not optional.
Emergency carePain or a broken tooth? Call before 10 am for a same-day slot.
Clear alignersA free consultation to see whether aligners are right for you.

Your first visit

  1. Fill in your forms onlineWe text you a link two days before. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Arrive ten minutes earlyBring your insurance card and a list of any medicines you take.
  3. Exam and a clear planWe show you what we see on the screen and explain every option and its price.
  4. Book what you needMost treatments can start the same week. Payment plans are available.
